Recommended

Allow lrclib.net through the filter

One change, and it stays fixed — for every device and every app on the network, with nothing sitting between you and the lyrics database.

  1. Open your router’s admin page, or your internet provider’s app.
  2. Find its content filter — often called safe browsing, advanced security, parental controls or managed sites.
  3. Add lrclib.net to its allowed list.
  4. Come back here and hit Try again.
Your provider’s security add-on
The most common cause. Managed in your provider’s own app or account page — not the router’s admin page. Look for a security or protection section with an allowed-sites list. Visiting the blocked site in a browser may also show a warning page with a “visit anyway” button, but that grant can quietly expire.
Router or gateway admin page
Look under Parental Controls, Managed Sites, Access Restrictions or Content Filtering.
Pi-hole, AdGuard Home, NextDNS or similar
Add lrclib.net to the allowlist.
Antivirus or security suite
Check its web-shield or HTTPS-scanning exclusions.
Work, school or campus network
You almost certainly can’t change this one. Use the mirror below.
If you can’t change the filter

Use the Cadence mirror

Sends lookups through a cached mirror on a different domain, so the filter has nothing to match on. Read-only, and your device details never reach the lyrics database. You can undo this any time in Settings › Lyrics.
